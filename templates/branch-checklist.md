# Branch Office Formation Checklist — Foreign Investor

> **Disclaimer:** This checklist is for general educational purposes only. It is not legal, financial, or regulatory advice. Requirements, fees, and timelines change. Always verify current requirements with the relevant authority and consult qualified professionals before proceeding.

**Entity type:** Branch of a Foreign Company (فرع شركة أجنبية)  
**Key characteristic:** A branch is not a separate legal entity. The foreign parent company bears full legal and financial responsibility for all of the branch's activities in Saudi Arabia.  
**Primary path:** MISA Investment Registration (branch path) → Single National Commercial Registration → post-registration compliance

---

## April 2025 CR Law Change — What Branch Entities Must Know

> **Royal Decree M/83 (April 2025)** abolished separate branch Commercial Registrations. Key impacts:
>
> - A **single national CR** now covers all branches and activities nationwide
> - New branch CRs are no longer issued — the parent entity's single CR applies
> - **Existing branch CRs** have a grace period until **April 2030** to convert or cancel
> - **Exception:** A branch CR of a foreign company remains valid if it is the **only Saudi-registered entity** of that foreign company
>
> For new foreign company branch registrations, a single CR is obtained after MISA registration — no separate branch CR is required or available.

---

## Key Differences: Branch vs. LLC

| Factor | Branch | LLC |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Legal entity | Not separate — extension of parent | Separate Saudi legal entity |
| Liability | Full parent liability | Limited to capital contribution |
| Foreign ownership | 100% (inherent — parent owns branch) | Sector-dependent (often 100% permitted) |
| MISA registration required | Yes | Yes |
| Commercial Registration | Single national CR (April 2025) | Single national CR (April 2025) |
| Activities | Must match parent company's activities | Defined in AoA and MISA registration |
| Profit repatriation | Simpler — branch profits flow to parent | Requires dividend distribution process |

## Pre-Checklist — Eligibility

- [ ] Applicant is an established foreign company (not an individual)
- [ ] Foreign company has a valid Commercial Registration in its home country
- [ ] Foreign company has audited financial statements for the last fiscal year
- [ ] Both documents are authenticated by the Saudi Embassy in the company's home country
- [ ] Board has resolved to establish a Saudi branch (board resolution required)

> **Note:** Individual foreign investors cannot establish a branch. A branch is, by definition, an extension of a foreign legal entity. If you are an individual, consider the LLC or Entrepreneurial Registration paths instead.

## Step 1 — MISA Investment Registration (Branch Path)

**Authority:** Ministry of Investment (MISA)  
**Portal:** misa.gov.sa  
**Timeline:** 1–10 business days (regulated sectors: longer)  
**Fee:** Determined by MISA after approval — not published in advance. Payment required within 15 business days of notification.

### Documents required

- [ ] Completed application via MISA e-portal (branch registration type)
- [ ] Foreign company Commercial Registration — authenticated by the Saudi Embassy
- [ ] Audited financial statements of the foreign parent (last fiscal year) — authenticated by the Saudi Embassy
- [ ] Articles of Association of the foreign parent company — authenticated by the Saudi Embassy
- [ ] Board Resolution of the foreign parent authorizing establishment of the Saudi branch — notarized and authenticated by the Saudi Embassy
- [ ] Power of Attorney appointing the branch manager in Saudi Arabia — notarized and authenticated
- [ ] Passport of the appointed branch manager

### Checklist

- [ ] Application submitted via MISA e-portal
- [ ] Fee notification received from MISA
- [ ] Fee paid within 15 business days of notification
- [ ] **Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) issued and active — branch type**

> **Common mistake:** Submitting documents that are not authenticated by the Saudi Embassy in the parent company's country. Notarization alone is not sufficient — Saudi Embassy authentication is mandatory.  
> **Tip:** Authentication requirements vary by country. Some countries are party to the Apostille Convention — verify the specific requirements with the Saudi Embassy in the parent company's jurisdiction.

## Step 2 — Trade Name and Business Address

**Authority:** Ministry of Commerce via Saudi Business Center (SBC)  
**Portal:** saudibc.gov.sa

### Checklist

- [ ] Branch name checked for availability (typically the foreign parent company name — confirm with SBC)
- [ ] Business premises identified and lease agreed with landlord
- [ ] Electronic tenancy contract registered on the Ejar platform (ejar.sa) — both landlord and tenant must be Ejar-registered
- [ ] **Ejari-registered electronic lease certificate obtained for the branch address**

> **Common mistake:** Using a paper tenancy contract. Only Ejari-registered electronic leases are accepted for CR purposes.

---

## Step 3 — Commercial Registration (CR)

**Authority:** Ministry of Commerce via Saudi Business Center (SBC)  
**Portal:** saudibc.gov.sa or mc.gov.sa  
**Timeline:** Same day to 7 business days (typical: 1 business day for standard activities)  
**Fee:** SAR 200/year (annual confirmation) + SAR 800+/year Chamber of Commerce membership

### Documents required

- [ ] Active MISA Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) — branch type
- [ ] Ejari-registered electronic lease for branch premises
- [ ] Board Resolution and Power of Attorney (from Step 1)

### Checklist

- [ ] CR application submitted via SBC portal
- [ ] CR annual confirmation fee paid (SAR 200/year)
- [ ] Chamber of Commerce membership fee paid (SAR 800+/year)
- [ ] **Single national Commercial Registration (CR) issued — 10-digit number starting with 7**

> **Note:** Under the April 2025 CR Law, this single CR covers the branch and all activities. No separate branch CR is issued.  
> See: `data/fees.en.json` — entries `commercial_registration_issuance_fee` and `chamber_of_commerce_fee`  
> See: `docs/en/commercial-registration.md`

## Step 5 — ZATCA Tax Registration

**Authority:** Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA)  
**Portal:** zatca.gov.sa  
**Timeline:** [placeholder — verify at zatca.gov.sa]  
**Fee:** [placeholder — verify whether ZATCA charges a registration fee]

> **Branch tax note:** A branch of a foreign company is subject to Saudi Corporate Income Tax (CIT) at 20% on Saudi-sourced income. The branch cannot be subject to Zakat (which applies only to Saudi and GCC shareholders of Saudi entities). Verify the applicable tax treatment with ZATCA and a qualified tax advisor.  
> See: `docs/en/tax-compliance.md`

### Checklist

- [ ] ZATCA registration submitted
- [ ] Tax Registration Number (TRN) received
- [ ] **ZATCA registration active**

## Step 8 — Muqeem (Expatriate Employees Only)

**Authority:** Ministry of Interior — General Directorate of Passports (Jawazat)  
**Portal:** muqeem.sa  
**Fee:** SAR 51.75/year per employee (Iqama issuance/renewal)

### Checklist

- [ ] Muqeem portal linked to employer CR and GOSI registration
- [ ] Work permit obtained for each expatriate employee
- [ ] Iqama application submitted within 90 days of each employee's arrival
- [ ] Iqama fee paid (SAR 51.75/year per employee)
- [ ] **Iqama issued for each expatriate employee**

> **Critical:** Iqama must be issued within 90 days of the employee's arrival. Late issuance triggers penalties.  
> See: `data/fees.en.json` — entries `iqama_issuance_renewal_fee`, `final_exit_visa_fee`, `exit_reentry_visa_extension_fee`

## Step 9 — Corporate Bank Account

**Authority:** SAMA-regulated Saudi bank  
**Timeline:** 7–28 business days from complete document submission  
**Fee:** No government fee

**Prerequisites before visiting the bank:**

- [ ] Active MISA Investment Registration Certificate (IRC)
- [ ] Active Commercial Registration (CR)
- [ ] National Address registered via Absher Business (absher.sa)
- [ ] Branch Manager Iqama — valid, not expired

> See: `docs/en/corporate-banking.md` for full bank selection and document requirements.

### Checklist

- [ ] Bank selected — confirm branch handles foreign-entity / branch onboarding
- [ ] Complete document package prepared
- [ ] KYC review completed
- [ ] **Corporate bank account active**
- [ ] WPS (Mudad) integration set up for salary payments if employees are hired

## Branch-Specific Considerations

| Topic | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Saudization (Nitaqat) | Branch employees are counted under the same Nitaqat rules as LLC employees. The branch manager (GM) Iqama is required for CR and bank operations. |
| Activity scope | Branch activities must be consistent with the MISA investment registration and the parent company's own activities. Activities outside the registered scope require a MISA amendment. |
| Repatriation of funds | Profits flow to the parent company. Withholding Tax (WHT) at 5% applies to dividends remitted to a non-resident parent — verify applicable double tax treaty at zatca.gov.sa. |
| Conversion to LLC | Branches can be converted to an LLC if the business structure needs change. This is a separate MISA and MC process — seek legal advice before conversion. |
| Existing branch CRs | If the foreign parent previously held a Saudi branch CR (pre-April 2025), that CR must be converted to the single national CR or cancelled before April 2030. |

## Common Mistakes Summary

| Stage | Common Mistake |
|-------|---------------|
| Authentication | Documents notarized but not authenticated by the Saudi Embassy |
| MISA fee | Assuming the fee is zero or a fixed amount — it is determined after approval |
| Electronic lease | Using a paper tenancy contract instead of Ejari |
| Branch CR | Attempting to obtain a separate branch CR — abolished April 2025 |
| Balady license | Commencing operations at the branch before the municipality license is issued |
| Iqama deadline | Missing the 90-day Iqama issuance deadline for expatriate staff |
| Bank prerequisites | Visiting the bank before all prerequisites (IRC, CR, National Address, Iqama) are in place |
