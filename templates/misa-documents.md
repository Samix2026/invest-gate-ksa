# MISA Registration Document Requirements

> **Disclaimer:** This document is for general educational purposes only. It is not legal, financial, or regulatory advice. Document requirements change. Always verify the current document checklist directly with MISA at [misa.gov.sa](https://misa.gov.sa) before submitting an application.

**Source:** MISA Investor Guide, 12th Edition, Section 3.1.1 (March/July 2025)  
**Portal:** misa.gov.sa

---

## Overview

MISA offers multiple registration pathways for foreign investors. The required documents differ significantly by pathway. Identify your applicable pathway before preparing documents.

| Pathway | Who It Is For |
|---------|--------------|
| Standard Investment Registration | Foreign companies (established 1+ year) establishing a Saudi entity |
| Branch Registration | Foreign companies establishing a Saudi branch (not a new local entity) |
| GCC National Registration | Citizens of GCC member states |
| Premium Residency Pathway | Holders of Saudi Premium Residency |
| Entrepreneurial Registration | Foreign individuals without an established foreign company — must be innovative/tech-enabled |

---

## Pathway 1 — Standard Investment Registration (Foreign Company)

**Eligible:** A foreign company that has been established for at least 1 year, with a valid CR and audited financial statements in its home country.

**Documents authenticated by the Saudi Embassy** means the document has been notarized in the home country AND then stamped/authenticated by the Saudi Embassy in that country. Notarization alone is not sufficient.

| Document | Authentication Required | Notes |
|----------|------------------------|-------|
| Application form | None — submitted via MISA e-portal | Complete all required fields online at misa.gov.sa |
| Foreign company Commercial Registration (CR) | Yes — Saudi Embassy authentication | Must be current and valid in the home country |
| Audited financial statements (last fiscal year) | Yes — Saudi Embassy authentication | Prepared by a licensed auditor in the home country |
| Articles of Association of the foreign parent | Yes — Saudi Embassy authentication | Full articles; translated to Arabic where required by MISA |
| Board Resolution authorizing the Saudi investment | Yes — notarized + Saudi Embassy authentication | Resolution must specifically authorize the Saudi investment and name any authorized representative |
| Power of Attorney for the authorized representative in Saudi Arabia | Yes — notarized + Saudi Embassy authentication | Names and empowers the individual who will act on behalf of the company in Saudi Arabia |
| Passport of the authorized representative | Copy only — no authentication required | Must be valid |

> **GCC member states:** Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, UAE. Citizens of these states follow a different pathway (see Pathway 3 below).

### Common Issues

- Document is authenticated by the home country's foreign ministry but not by the Saudi Embassy — this is insufficient
- Audited financials are for a fiscal year more than 12 months old — MISA may require updated statements
- Board Resolution is generic or does not specifically reference the Saudi investment
- Authorized representative is named in the Power of Attorney but the PoA has expired

## Pathway 2 — Branch Registration (Foreign Company Branch)

**Eligible:** Foreign companies establishing a branch in Saudi Arabia (not incorporating a new local entity). The branch is not a separate legal entity — the parent bears full liability.

| Document | Authentication Required | Notes |
|----------|------------------------|-------|
| Application form | None — submitted via MISA e-portal | Select "branch registration" type |
| Foreign company Commercial Registration (CR) | Yes — Saudi Embassy authentication | Parent company's home country CR |
| Audited financial statements of parent (last fiscal year) | Yes — Saudi Embassy authentication | Prepared by licensed auditor in home country |
| Articles of Association of the foreign parent | Yes — Saudi Embassy authentication | Full articles of the parent company |
| Board Resolution authorizing the Saudi branch | Yes — notarized + Saudi Embassy authentication | Must specifically authorize establishment of a Saudi branch |
| Power of Attorney appointing the branch manager in Saudi Arabia | Yes — notarized + Saudi Embassy authentication | Empowers the named individual to manage the Saudi branch |
| Passport of the appointed branch manager | Copy only | Must be valid |

> **April 2025 note:** Under Royal Decree M/83, a single national CR now covers the branch and all activities. No separate branch CR is issued. See `docs/en/commercial-registration.md`.

## Pathway 3 — GCC National Registration

**Eligible:** Citizens of Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, or the UAE. Saudi nationals are not foreign investors and do not use the MISA pathway.

**GCC nationals register directly with the Ministry of Commerce** and do not require a MISA investment registration for most activities. The standard LLC or business formation documents for the Ministry of Commerce apply.

| Document | Authentication Required | Notes |
|----------|------------------------|-------|
| GCC national identity card or passport | Copy only | Must clearly show GCC nationality |
| Applicable Ministry of Commerce documents | Per MC requirements | See Saudi Business Center: saudibc.gov.sa |

> **Verify with MISA and the Ministry of Commerce** whether your specific activity or sector requires MISA involvement for GCC nationals. Some regulated sectors may have different requirements.

---

## Pathway 4 — Saudi Premium Residency Pathway

**Eligible:** Holders of Saudi Premium Residency (Iqama Mumayyaz) investing in their personal capacity.

**Document waiver:** Saudi Premium Residency holders are **exempt from the standard MISA authentication requirements**. The Premium Residency certificate substitutes for the foreign company CR and audited financials requirement.

| Document | Authentication Required | Notes |
|----------|------------------------|-------|
| Application form | None — submitted via MISA e-portal | |
| Saudi Premium Residency certificate | None — issued by Saudi government | Must be valid and active |
| Passport | Copy only | |

> **Verify with MISA** the full current document list for Premium Residency applicants at misa.gov.sa. Requirements may be updated.

## Pathway 5 — Entrepreneurial Registration

**Eligible:** Foreign individuals without an established foreign company who are establishing an innovative, tech-enabled, or Vision 2030-aligned business in Saudi Arabia. This pathway is **not available** for standard services companies or traditional microbusinesses.

| Document | Authentication Required | Notes |
|----------|------------------------|-------|
| Application form | None — submitted via MISA e-portal | Select "entrepreneurial registration" type |
| Support letter from MISA-recognized incubator, accelerator, or VC | None — issued by the supporting organization | The supporting organization must be recognized by MISA; verify the list at misa.gov.sa |
| Business plan | None | Must demonstrate the innovative, tech-enabled, or Vision 2030-aligned nature of the business |
| Passport of the applicant | Copy only | Must be valid |

> **Key restriction:** The Entrepreneurial Registration applies only to innovative/tech-enabled/high-growth businesses aligned with Vision 2030 sectors (FinTech, HealthTech, AI, renewable energy, tourism, healthcare, etc.). Standard IT services, consulting, or traditional microbusiness activities do not qualify.  
> See: `docs/en/registration-types.md` — Entrepreneurial Registration section.

**Fee structure (Entrepreneurial path):**

| Period | Fee |
|--------|-----|
| Years 1–3 | SAR 2,000/year (waiver of standard fee) |
| Years 4–5 | SAR 12,000/year |
| Year 6+ | SAR 62,000/year (standard rate applies) |

> Source: Wamda/Tawaref 2024, TASC 2025. Cross-reference: `data/fees.en.json` — entries `entrepreneurial_license_fee_y1_3` and `entrepreneurial_license_fee_y4_5`.

## Authentication Process — How It Works

For documents requiring Saudi Embassy authentication:

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Obtain the original document in the home country |
| 2 | Have the document notarized by a notary public in the home country |
| 3 | Have the notarized document authenticated by the home country's Ministry of Foreign Affairs (or equivalent) |
| 4 | Submit the document to the Saudi Embassy in the home country for Embassy authentication stamp |
| 5 | Translate to Arabic if required (confirm with MISA whether certified translation is needed) |

> Processing time and fees for Saudi Embassy authentication vary by country. Contact the Saudi Embassy in the parent company's home country directly to confirm current requirements and processing times.

## After MISA Approval — What Comes Next

| Step | Action | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Pay MISA fee | Within 15 business days of fee notification — failure voids the registration |
| 2 | Receive Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) | Active IRC is the prerequisite for Commercial Registration |
| 3 | Proceed to Commercial Registration | Via Saudi Business Center: saudibc.gov.sa |
| 4 | Annual Registration Update | Required each year under Investment Law Executive Regulations (Ministerial Decision 1086, February 2025) |
