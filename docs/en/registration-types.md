# MISA Registration Types for Foreign Investors

> **Disclaimer:** This document is for general educational purposes only. It is not legal, financial, regulatory, or tax advice. Requirements, procedures, fees, and eligibility rules change. Always verify current information with official Saudi government portals and consult qualified professionals before making any decision.

> **Status note:** As of February 2025, the traditional MISA License has been replaced by the Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) under the Investment Law (Royal Decree M/19, 2024) and its Implementing Regulations (Ministerial Decision 1086, February 2025). Foreign investors must register with MISA before commencing any investment activity in Saudi Arabia.

All entries below are **draft** status unless otherwise indicated. Verify current requirements, fees, and eligibility at [misa.gov.sa](https://misa.gov.sa) and [investsaudi.sa](https://investsaudi.sa) before proceeding.

## Registration Types

### 1. Standard Investment Registration

The standard investment registration (formerly "MISA License") is the foundational pathway for most foreign investors establishing an operating company in Saudi Arabia. It is processed via the MISA portal and authorizes the investor to establish a Saudi legal entity for the approved activities.

Key characteristics:
- Requires an established foreign parent company (typically 1+ year of operation)
- A single registration covers multiple sectors (since Investment Law 2024)
- A MISA investment registration is a prerequisite to commercial registration for foreign investors
- Registration fee determined by MISA post-approval — amount varies by activity type and is not published in advance

> **⚠️ Important — Who qualifies for standard registration:**
> Standard MISA Investment Registration requires a copy of the commercial registration of the applicant's foreign establishment, authenticated by the Saudi Embassy, plus audited financial statements for the last fiscal year.
>
> **Foreign individuals without an established foreign company do NOT qualify for standard registration.**
>
> Eligible applicants: foreign companies (established 1+ year), Premium Residency holders (documents waived), GCC nationals.
>
> Source: MISA Investor Guide 12th Edition, Section 3.1.1 (March 2025)

For the full setup sequence, see [docs/en/company-setup-overview.md](./company-setup-overview.md).

### 2. Regional Headquarters (RHQ) License

The RHQ program was launched in 2021 and allows multinational companies to establish their regional management hub in Saudi Arabia — primarily Riyadh. Co-managed by MISA and the Royal Commission for Riyadh City (RCRC). As of Q2 2025, 650+ companies are registered.

**Who it is for:** Multinational companies with legal presence and operations in at least 2 countries outside Saudi Arabia (excluding home country).

**Key restriction:** The RHQ cannot generate direct commercial revenue in Saudi Arabia. It covers regional management, strategy, budgeting, and marketing only. Commercial activities must be handled by a separately licensed MISA entity.

**Staffing obligations (mandatory):**
- By end of year 1: minimum 15 full-time employees
- Including: minimum 3 senior executives with prior experience at the group's global or regional headquarters

**Fees:** Fee structure not confirmed from official government source. Multiple commercial sources cite SAR 10,000 for year 1 and SAR 2,000/year from year 2, but these amounts have not been verified in the MISA Investor Guide 12th Ed. (July 2025) or on the investsaudi.sa portal. Verify directly via the MISA e-portal before budgeting.

**Timeline:** Application submitted online via MISA portal. Processing: 5–10 working days. Mandatory activities must commence within 6 months of licensing.

**Key benefits:**
- Eligible for 0% corporate income tax rate on qualifying RHQ activities (subject to conditions — verify at investsaudi.sa)
- Exempt from Saudization (Nitaqat) for RHQ-specific functions
- Government contract eligibility requires active RHQ license (mandatory since January 2024)

> **[PLACEHOLDER]** Verify current RHQ fee schedule, eligibility requirements, and approved activity list directly at [investsaudi.sa](https://investsaudi.sa) or via the MISA e-portal.

### 3. Entrepreneurial License

The Entrepreneurial License is a MISA pathway for foreign entrepreneurs and innovative startups to launch a company directly in Saudi Arabia — without requiring an established foreign parent company or high capital. It is part of Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 startup ecosystem.

> **Who should consider Entrepreneurial Registration:**
> This is the primary pathway for foreign individuals who do not have an established foreign company but wish to launch an innovative business in Saudi Arabia. Requirements: support letter from a MISA-recognized incubator, accelerator, or VC fund. Not suitable for traditional businesses, retail, or most regulated sectors (healthcare, real estate, financial services).

**Who it is for:** Foreign entrepreneurs and innovative startups meeting eligibility criteria below.

**Eligibility (one of the following must apply):**
- VC-backed startup (Venture Capital firm recognized by MISA)
- Incubator or accelerator program participant (recognized by MISA)
- Patent holder for an innovative product or idea
- Support letter from a recognized government entity (MISA, PIF, KAUST, TDF)

**Business must be:**
- Innovative, tech-enabled, or high-growth
- Aligned with Vision 2030 sectors (FinTech, HealthTech, AI, renewable energy, tourism, healthcare)
- Not a standard IT services company or traditional microbusiness

**Required documents:**
- Support letter from an approved incubator, VC, or accelerator (mandatory)
- Business plan demonstrating innovation and Vision 2030 alignment
- Passport copies of all founders and shareholders
- If founders hold Saudi Iqama: NOC from current sponsor (Chamber-attested)

**Fees:**

| Period | Annual Fee |
|--------|-----------|
| Years 1–3 | SAR 2,000/year (waiver of SAR 60,000 standard fee) |
| Years 4–5 | SAR 12,000/year |
| Year 6+ | SAR 62,000/year (standard MISA fee applies) |

**Timeline:** 5–7 business days after complete documentation is submitted.

**Key benefits:**
- No minimum capital for most sectors
- No established foreign parent company required
- 100% foreign ownership permitted
- Access to premium residency if startup raises SAR 400,000+ from authorized parties
- Path to permanent residency if raises $4M and employs 10 staff for 1 year

> **[PLACEHOLDER]** Verify current eligibility criteria, recognized incubator/accelerator list, and application requirements at [misa.gov.sa](https://misa.gov.sa).

### 4. Temporary Registration

> **Status: Unconfirmed under 2025 framework**

Under the pre-2025 system, MISA offered a temporary license category for investors commencing activities before a full registration was complete. Under the new Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) framework (Investment Law, Royal Decree M/19, 2024 / Implementing Regulations, February 2025), this pathway has not been confirmed as a distinct active track in official documentation.

This entry is recorded as a source gap only. See `data/source-gaps.en.json` (id: `temporary_registration`) for verification status and next steps.
