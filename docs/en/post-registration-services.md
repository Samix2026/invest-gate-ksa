# Post-Registration Services — Ministry of Investment (MISA)

> **Source:** MISA Investor Guide, 12th Edition, March 2025
> **Verification date:** 2026-05-20
> **Status:** Verified against official source

## Overview

After completing investment registration, MISA offers value-added programs and services to support investors throughout their operational journey in Saudi Arabia. These services are provided directly by MISA or through its regulated portals and do not replace the standard registration, licensing, or compliance steps.

---

## Miza Program

The Miza Program is a free, value-added services platform under the National Investment Strategy. It connects investors — both local and foreign — with private sector business service providers via a regulated electronic portal.

**Who can use it:** Local and foreign investors  
**Cost:** Free (SAR 0 — confirmed)  
**Access:** MISA electronic portal — investsaudi.sa  
**Source:** MISA Investor Guide 12th Edition, §3.3.2

| # | Portfolio | What it covers |
|---|-----------|----------------|
| 1 | Communication and Events | Event management, investor communications, networking facilitation |
| 2 | Talent Solutions | Recruitment support, HR advisory, workforce planning |
| 3 | Market Entry Support | Business development assistance, market access guidance |
| 4 | Legal, Tax, and Compliance Services | Legal advisory, tax consultation, regulatory compliance |
| 5 | Market Analysis | Sector research, market intelligence, competitive analysis |
| 6 | Real Estate Services | Commercial property sourcing, lease advisory |
| 7 | Financial and Accounting Services | CFO services, accounting, financial reporting |

## Strategic Investor Program

The Strategic Investor Program is a premium support package for national and foreign companies that meet eligibility criteria. It aims to enhance the investment environment and create new opportunities for qualifying investors.

**Who qualifies:** Strategic companies with national or foreign presence, companies registered in prominent government programs, and companies not yet registered in the Kingdom that meet eligibility criteria  
**Cost:** Determined post-classification (may be zero for some classifications)  
**Processing:** 5 working days after complete application submission  
**Access:** investsaudi.sa — Strategic Investor Program portal  
**Source:** MISA Investor Guide 12th Edition, §3.3.1

### Key Benefits

- Support for investment opportunity development
- Specialized economic sector data and information
- Investment reports tailored to foreign countries
- Advisory support on legislation and regulations
- Support for issuing professional and operational licenses
- Guidance on industrial and economic zones
- Consultation on visa requirements and Saudization
- Support for finding Saudi talent (via Miza Program)
- Facilitation of premium residency
- Dedicated relationship manager
- Priority access to events and decision-makers
- Membership in other government programs

### Eligibility Criteria

- Companies registered in prominent government programs
- Companies with significant economic impact (by size or strategic sector)
- Strategic companies: national companies, foreign companies registered with MISA, or foreign companies not yet registered in the Kingdom

### Fee Note

The fee is determined by MISA upon classification approval and is not published in advance. It is payable within 30 days of notification. May be zero for some investor classifications. Confirm the applicable fee directly via investsaudi.sa before applying.

## One-Stop-Shop Centers

MISA operates integrated investor service centers in three cities:

- **Riyadh**
- **Jeddah**
- **Dammam**

Services available at these centers include government departments from:

- Ministry of Justice
- Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD)
- Ministry of Interior (Passports and Recruitment)
- General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI)
- Ministry of Foreign Affairs

These centers allow investors to complete multiple government procedures in a single visit.
