# Commercial Registration (CR) — Guide for Foreign Investors

> **Disclaimer:** This document is for general educational purposes only. It is not legal, financial, or regulatory advice. Requirements, fees, and procedures change. Always verify current information directly with the Ministry of Commerce at [mc.gov.sa](https://www.mc.gov.sa) and the Saudi Business Center at [saudibc.gov.sa](https://www.saudibc.gov.sa).

---

## What Is a Commercial Registration?

A Commercial Registration (CR) — السجل التجاري — is the official business identity document issued by the Ministry of Commerce. It authorizes a legal entity to conduct commercial activities in Saudi Arabia and establishes the entity's legal existence in the Kingdom.

For foreign investors, obtaining a CR is the second major step in the setup sequence:

1. MISA Investment Registration (prerequisite for foreign investors)
2. **Commercial Registration ← this guide**
3. Municipality Operating License (Balady)
4. Tax registration (ZATCA)
5. Labor and social insurance registration (GOSI, Qiwa, Muqeem)

A CR number alone does not authorize operations from a physical premises, employment of staff, or collection of VAT — each requires its own subsequent step.

---

## April 2025 Law Change — Royal Decree M/83

The Commercial Registration Law enacted by Royal Decree M/83 in April 2025 restructured the CR system. Key changes that apply to all new registrations and existing companies:

| Change | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Single National CR | One CR now covers all activities and all branches nationwide |
| Permanent CR | CR has no expiry date — annual data confirmation replaces periodic renewal |
| Branch CRs abolished | Separate branch CRs no longer issued; existing branch CRs are in a grace period |
| New CR number format | 10 digits starting with the digit 7 (replaces old regional codes) |
| Ejari lease mandatory | Electronic lease registration required; paper tenancy contracts not accepted |
| Grace period | Existing branch CRs valid until April 2030 — must convert or cancel before then |

> **Exception:** A branch CR of a foreign company remains valid beyond April 2030 only if it is the sole Saudi-registered entity of that foreign company.

---

## CR Number Format

All CR numbers issued under the April 2025 system are 10 digits and begin with the digit **7**. Old CR numbers starting with regional codes (1, 2, 4, etc.) were issued under the prior system and remain valid during the transition period.

## Required Documents

| Document | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| MISA Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) | Foreign investors only — must be active before applying for CR |
| Trade name reservation | Reserved via the Saudi Business Center (SBC) portal before application |
| Electronic lease (Ejari) | Mandatory — registered through the Ejar platform at the business address |
| Articles of Association (AoA) | Drafted and notarized in Arabic for the new entity |
| Partner / shareholder identification | Valid passports; Iqama if in-Kingdom |

> Verify the current document checklist at [mc.gov.sa](https://www.mc.gov.sa) or [saudibc.gov.sa](https://www.saudibc.gov.sa) before submission. Additional documents may be required depending on activity type and entity structure.

## Step-by-Step Process — Saudi Business Center Portal

| Step | Action | Notes |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Reserve trade name | Via Saudi Business Center (SBC) portal — [saudibc.gov.sa](https://www.saudibc.gov.sa) |
| 2 | Register electronic lease (Ejari) | Business address must have an active Ejar-registered tenancy; paper contracts not accepted |
| 3 | Draft and notarize Articles of Association | Arabic-language AoA for the entity being formed |
| 4 | Submit CR application | Online via SBC portal; link MISA IRC, Ejari lease, AoA, and trade name |
| 5 | Pay fees | SAR 200/year CR annual confirmation + SAR 800+/year Chamber of Commerce fee |
| 6 | CR issued | Immediate to 7 business days depending on activity type (see Timeline section) |

## Fees

| Fee | Amount | Frequency | Source |
|-----|--------|-----------|--------|
| CR annual confirmation | SAR 200 | Annual | Ministry of Commerce fee schedule (2025) |
| Chamber of Commerce membership | SAR 800 minimum | Annual | Mandatory for all registered companies; Class 3 minimum rate |

**Chamber of Commerce fee:** All Saudi-registered companies must hold active Chamber of Commerce membership. The SAR 800/year figure is the minimum Class 3 rate. Larger companies fall into higher classes with fees of SAR 10,000/year or more. Membership is required for CR issuance and the annual confirmation cycle.

## Annual Confirmation

Under Royal Decree M/83, the CR does not expire. However, the registered entity must complete an annual data confirmation cycle:

- Confirm or update all registered information (address, activities, partners)
- Pay the SAR 200 annual fee
- Failure to complete the confirmation cycle can result in the CR being flagged as inactive, blocking further government services

This is sometimes informally called "CR renewal." It is technically an annual data confirmation, not a renewal of a time-limited license.

---

## Branch CR Grace Period (Existing Entities)

Under the pre-April 2025 system, companies with multiple locations held separate branch CRs (SAR 100/year each). The April 2025 law abolished branch CRs for new registrations and placed existing ones in a structured transition:

| Status | Details |
|--------|---------|
| New branch CRs | No longer issued — a single national CR covers all branches |
| Existing branch CRs | Remain valid until April 2030 |
| Required action | Convert to single national CR or cancel branch CR before April 2030 |
| Exception | Foreign company branch CR remains valid if it is the only Saudi-registered entity of that foreign company |

---

## What CR Does Not Authorize

Holding a CR number does not by itself authorize:

- **Operating from a physical location** — a Municipality Operating License (Balady license) is required for each business premises
- **Employing staff** — GOSI registration and Qiwa employer registration are required before hiring
- **Collecting VAT** — ZATCA VAT registration is required once taxable turnover meets the mandatory threshold
- **Opening a bank account** — the active CR is a prerequisite for corporate banking, but the bank account is a separate process
