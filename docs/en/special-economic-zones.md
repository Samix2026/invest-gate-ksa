# Special Economic Zones (SEZs) in Saudi Arabia

> **Educational reference only.** This document describes the structure of Saudi SEZs for informational purposes. It is not legal advice, tax advice, or a guarantee of specific incentives. Verify all figures directly with ECZA before making investment decisions.

---

## Overview

Saudi Arabia operates five Special Economic Zones (SEZs) governed by the **Economic Cities and Special Zones Authority (ECZA)**. SEZs are demarcated areas within Saudi Arabia's customs territory that offer preferential tax and regulatory conditions to attract targeted foreign investment.

The legal basis for SEZ regulations is **Cabinet Decision No. 468/1447**, published in the Official Gazette on **16 January 2026**, effective **16 April 2026** (90 days after publication).

> **Important:** SILZ (Special Integrated Logistics Zone) operates under a **completely different tax framework** from the three industrial zones. SILZ offers **0% CIT for 50 years** — not 5% for 20 years. See the SILZ section below.

---

## SEZ vs. MODON Industrial City

| Feature | SEZ — Industrial Zones (KAEC/Jazan/Ras Al-Khair) | SILZ | MODON Industrial City |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governing body | ECZA | ECZA / GACA (licensing) | MODON |
| CIT rate | 5% | **0%** | Standard 20% |
| CIT duration | 20 years | **50 years** | Not applicable |
| WHT on profit repatriation | 0% | 0% | Standard rates |
| Customs on capital equipment | 0% | 0% (suspended) | Standard rates |
| Intra-zone VAT | 0% | 0% (suspended) | Standard 15% |
| Foreign ownership | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| Nitaqat exemption | 5 years (relaxed) | 5 years | Standard Nitaqat applies |

## Foundational Tax Rule (Industrial Zones)

> **All entities in industrial SEZs (KAEC, Jazan, Ras Al-Khair) pay CIT at 5% — regardless of ownership nationality.**

On mainland Saudi Arabia, Saudi and GCC entities pay **Zakat** (not CIT) and foreign-owned entities pay **CIT at 20%**. In industrial SEZs, this rule is reversed: **every entity pays CIT at 5%**, whether it is Saudi-owned, GCC-owned, or foreign-owned. Zakat does not apply within an SEZ.

**SILZ exception:** SILZ applies **0% CIT for 50 years** under the ZATCA SILZ Guideline (December 2023) — a fundamentally different framework from the industrial zones.

| CIT rate | 5% for 20 years |

| Foreign ownership | 100% |

- Automobile supply chain and assembly
- Consumer goods manufacturing
- Electronic light manufacturing
- Pharmaceutical manufacturing
- Medical technology (MedTech)
- Logistics and warehousing
- ICT and technology services

### 5. Special Integrated Logistics Zone (SILZ)

> **CRITICAL: SILZ operates under a completely different tax framework from the industrial zones above.**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Location | King Khalid International Airport logistics corridor, Riyadh |
| Area | ~3.0 km² |
| Target sectors | Logistics, freight forwarding, e-commerce fulfillment, air cargo, supply chain, consumer electronics, pharmaceuticals |
| CIT rate | **0% for 50 years** (not 5% for 20 years) |
| WHT | **0%** during tax holiday period |
| Customs (intra-zone / from ports) | **Suspended** within zone |
| Intra-zone VAT | **Suspended** within zone |
| Foreign ownership | 100% |
| Share capital | No minimum requirement |
| Profit repatriation | No restrictions |
| Nitaqat relaxation | 5 years |
| Regulatory authority (licensing) | **GACA** (General Authority of Civil Aviation) — not ECZA |
| First occupant | Apple Inc (regional distribution center) |
| Verification | Verified — ZATCA SILZ Guideline (Dec 2023) + EY Tax Alert + Aurifer Tax (Mar 2026) + Cabinet Decision No. 468/1447 |

**SILZ Tax Framework — Different from Industrial Zones**

SILZ operates under a distinct regulatory framework:
- **CIT: 0% for 50 years** (vs 5% for 20 years in KAEC/Jazan/Ras Al-Khair)
- **WHT: 0%** during the tax holiday period
- **VAT and customs: suspended** for goods within the zone and transferred from ports
- **Regulatory authority: GACA** (not ECZA for licensing)
- **Qualifying condition**: investor must have presence in at least 3 countries regionally or globally
- Source: ZATCA SILZ Guideline (December 2023) + ECZA regulations (April 2026, Cabinet Decision No. 468/1447)

> **Note:** Goods exiting SILZ to the Saudi mainland are subject to standard customs duties and VAT at mainland rates. SILZ incentives apply to intra-zone activities and re-exports outside Saudi Arabia.

**Strategic advantage:** Co-located with Riyadh's primary international airport, enabling same-day air cargo processing and regional distribution for the Saudi and Gulf market.

**Qualifying activities:**
- Maintenance, repair, processing, and assembly of goods
- Sorting, packing, repacking, trading, and distribution
- Importing, exporting, and re-exporting goods
- Value-added logistics services
- After-sales services
- Recycling and electronic waste management
- Simple manufacturing processes

**Target sectors:** Consumer electronics, computer products, pharmaceuticals, aviation technology, luxury goods, supply chain and distribution.

## Common Incentives Summary (Industrial Zones Only)

The following applies to **KAEC SEZ, Jazan SEZ, and Ras Al-Khair SEZ** (verified). **SILZ is NOT included in this table** — SILZ has a distinct and more favorable framework (0% CIT for 50 years).

| Incentive | Rate / Term |
|---|---|
| Corporate Income Tax (CIT) | 5% |
| CIT preferential period | 20 years |
| Withholding tax on profit repatriation | 0% |
| VAT on intra-zone goods transactions | 0% |
| Customs on capital equipment and production inputs | 0% |
| Maximum foreign ownership | 100% |
| Nitaqat (Saudization) relaxation period | 5 years |

**After the incentive period ends**, standard Saudi rates apply: CIT reverts to 20%, and WHT, VAT, and customs revert to standard mainland rates.

## Important Caveats

1. **Zone-to-mainland sales**: Moving goods from an SEZ to the Saudi mainland triggers standard mainland tax and customs rates. SEZ incentives apply to intra-zone activities and exports outside Saudi Arabia.

2. **Approved activities lists**: Each zone has a specific list of qualifying business activities. Not all sectors are eligible for every zone. Detailed activity codes require direct verification with ECZA or GACA (for SILZ) before relying on SEZ incentives.

3. **Land costs not published**: ECZA does not publish a standard land rental rate schedule. Lease terms are negotiated with zone development entities on a case-by-case basis.

4. **SEZ vs. mainland structure**: Establishing in an SEZ requires committing to zone-specific operational constraints. Review zone bylaws and ECZA regulations with qualified legal counsel before proceeding.

5. **Zakat vs. CIT**: Entities in industrial SEZs (KAEC, Jazan, Ras Al-Khair) pay CIT — not Zakat — regardless of ownership structure. SILZ entities pay 0% CIT during the 50-year holiday period.

6. **SILZ eligibility**: SILZ requires investors to have a regional or global presence in at least 3 countries and to provide logistics services. It is not a general logistics zone open to any investor.
