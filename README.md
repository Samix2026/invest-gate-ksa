# Invest Gate KSA

A structured, bilingual knowledge repository for foreign investors looking to understand how to invest, establish, and operate businesses in Saudi Arabia.

> **Disclaimer:** This repository contains general educational information only. It is **not legal, financial, or tax advice**. Always consult licensed professionals and verify information against official government sources before making any decisions.

---

## What This Is

Invest Gate KSA is an open-source framework that organizes publicly available information about:

- Business structures available to foreign investors
- Licensing and registration processes
- Regulatory bodies and their roles
- Economic zones and incentive programs
- Sector-specific entry requirements
- Operational considerations (banking, visas, labor)

The project is designed to be:
- **Bilingual** — Arabic and English throughout
- **Source-linked** — every claim points to an official or verifiable source
- **AI-ready** — structured for use as a knowledge base, MCP server, or assistant context
- **Community-maintained** — open to contributions from practitioners and researchers
